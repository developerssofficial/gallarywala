Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 11, 2026

Welcome to GallaryWala ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at https://gallarywala.vercel.app.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways:

  • Personal Data: When you register an account or sign in with Google OAuth, we collect your name, email address, and avatar image.
  • User-Generated Content: Images, titles, descriptions, categories, and tags you upload to GallaryWala.
  • Device & Usage Data: Browser type, operating system, access times, and pages viewed directly on our platform to optimize user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the GallaryWala visual inspiration platform.
  • Authenticate users via Supabase and Google OAuth securely.
  • Display creator profiles and author attributions on uploaded photographs and artwork.
  • Prevent fraudulent activities, spam, and abuse.

3. Google User Data & OAuth Policy

GallaryWala's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We only access your basic profile (name, email, and profile picture) solely for account authentication and user display.

4. Data Storage & Security

We implement strict administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information. Media assets are securely stored via Cloudinary CDN, and user credentials are encrypted via Supabase authentication.

5. Third-Party Services

We may share data with reliable third-party infrastructure providers that enable our application to operate:

  • Supabase: For database management and secure user authentication.
  • Cloudinary: For secure media hosting and delivery.
  • Vercel: For cloud hosting and edge deployment.
